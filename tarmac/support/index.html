About Tarmac

Tarmac is a lightweight aircraft arrival scheduling utility designed to help aviation professionals organise upcoming aircraft arrivals and stay aware of scheduled arrival times. Each active aircraft can appear as a Live Activity on your Lock Screen, in order of arrival.

Important operational information

Tarmac is a manually managed scheduling and awareness utility. It does not provide live aircraft tracking, verified flight information, air traffic control services or operational clearance. Arrival times are only those you enter. Do not rely on Tarmac as the sole source of information for safety-critical aviation decisions.

Getting started

  1. Enter the aircraft registration, for example VH-ABC or N465DG.
  2. Optionally enter the aircraft type. If Tarmac remembers the registration, the type fills in for you.
  3. Optionally enter inbound and outbound identifiers.
  4. Choose Today or Tomorrow.
  5. Enter the expected arrival time in 24-hour format, for example 0815 for 08:15.
  6. Tap Add Manual Job. The aircraft appears in your list and as a Live Activity.
  7. When the arrival is finished, tap Done on the aircraft to remove it.

Frequently asked questions

How many aircraft can I add?

Up to five active aircraft. Overdue aircraft still count. A slot frees up when you mark an aircraft Done or when an old entry expires.

Why can’t I enter a time that has already passed?

New arrivals must be in the future, so a stale time is never shown as upcoming. For an arrival after midnight, choose Tomorrow; Tarmac never moves a Today time to Tomorrow by itself.

How do I correct an entry or a changed arrival time?

Entries cannot be edited. Mark the existing aircraft Done, then add it again with the correct details.

What is Fleet Memory?

When you add an aircraft with a type, Tarmac remembers that type for the registration. The next time you enter the same registration, the type fills in automatically. Fleet Memory stays on your iPhone.

How do I update or forget a remembered aircraft type?

To update it, add the aircraft with the new type. To forget it, enter the registration, clear the type field and add the aircraft.

How do I clear all remembered aircraft?

In Tarmac, open Settings and choose Wipe Fleet Memory Cache, then confirm. Your active aircraft are not affected.

Does Tarmac need an internet connection?

No. Tarmac works fully offline and makes no network requests. Only the Privacy Policy and Support links in Settings need a connection, because they open this website in your browser.

Where do Live Activities appear?

On the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island on iPhone models that have it, and on a paired Apple Watch where iOS supports it. If none appear, check that Live Activities are turned on for Tarmac in the iPhone Settings app.

Why did a Live Activity stop updating or disappear?

iOS limits how long a Live Activity keeps updating, roughly eight hours, and may remove it from the Lock Screen a few hours later. Opening Tarmac refreshes them. When an aircraft passes its arrival time, its Live Activity changes to OVERDUE; this can take up to about a minute.

What are notifications used for?

Tarmac asks for permission when you add your first aircraft. If you allow it, Tarmac sends a single local reminder, about seven and a half hours after it last refreshed your Lock Screen, asking you to open the app so your Live Activities stay current. Tarmac’s Settings screen shows whether notifications are on, and you can change this in the iPhone Settings app.

When are old entries removed?

Automatically, once they are more than 12 hours past their scheduled arrival, the next time Tarmac is open.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17.0 or later.

Is there a subscription?

No. Tarmac is a one-time purchase from the App Store, with no subscriptions and no in-app purchases. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple.

Contact support

Email support@tinkerstack.au. To help us help you, please include:

  • Tarmac’s version and build number, shown in Tarmac under Settings, App Information.
  • Your iOS version and iPhone model.
  • What happened, what you expected, and the steps that lead to it if you know them.
  • A screenshot if useful, with personal and operational details removed.

Please do not send passwords, payment details or confidential operational records. We reply by email; no fixed response time is promised. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting, as its data is stored only on your iPhone.
