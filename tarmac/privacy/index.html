Tarmac privacy policy

OverviewPrivacy policySupport

Effective 26 November 2026.

Tarmac is an iPhone app for keeping track of expected aircraft arrivals, published by TinkerStack Pty Ltd, Australia (“we”, “us”). It works offline. This policy explains what Tarmac stores on your iPhone and what information, if any, reaches us.

In short

  • What you enter in Tarmac stays on your iPhone. We do not collect it, and we cannot see it.
  • Tarmac has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Tarmac makes no network requests and uses no external flight data services.
  • We only receive information you choose to send us, for example when you email support.

Information stored on your device

Tarmac keeps the following in its own storage on your iPhone, where only Tarmac can use it:

  • Aircraft entries you create: the aircraft registration, aircraft type, inbound and outbound identifiers, the scheduled arrival date and time, and when the entry was created.
  • Fleet Memory: a list matching aircraft registrations to the aircraft types you have entered, so the type can fill in automatically next time. It is kept in iOS’s standard app preferences storage (UserDefaults).
  • Preferences: your appearance setting (match iPhone, light or dark), also kept in UserDefaults.

If Tarmac cannot read its saved file, or upgrades a file from an older version, it may keep a copy of the original file on your iPhone so that nothing is lost.

Tarmac uses iOS’s UserDefaults only for its own information, never to share it with other apps. This matches the reason declared in Tarmac’s privacy manifest.

Information we collect

Tarmac does not send any of the information above to us or to anyone else. We do not operate a server or a cloud database for Tarmac, and there is no Tarmac account. Tarmac contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software, and no third-party software development kits.

Tarmac does not track aircraft. It does not connect to flight tracking, air traffic or any other external data service. Every arrival time is one you entered.

Live Activities and the Dynamic Island

Each active aircraft is shown as a Live Activity using Apple’s ActivityKit. Its registration, aircraft type, inbound and outbound identifiers and arrival time can appear on your Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island on iPhone models that have it, and anywhere else iOS presents Live Activities, such as a paired Apple Watch. iOS creates these presentations on your devices; Tarmac does not send them anywhere.

Live Activities on the Lock Screen can be seen without unlocking your iPhone. You can turn them off for Tarmac in the iPhone Settings app.

Notifications

When you add your first aircraft, Tarmac asks permission to send notifications. If you allow them, Tarmac schedules a local reminder on your iPhone, about seven and a half hours after it last refreshed your Lock Screen, asking you to open the app so that your Live Activities stay current. The reminder contains no aircraft details. Tarmac does not use push notifications and sends no device token to us. You can change notification permission at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

How long information is kept

  • Marking an aircraft Done removes it from Tarmac.
  • Aircraft entries are removed automatically once they are more than 12 hours past their scheduled arrival, the next time Tarmac is open.
  • iOS removes Live Activities from the Lock Screen on its own schedule.
  • Fleet Memory and your preferences stay until you change or remove them, or delete the app.

Your choices

  • Forget one remembered aircraft type: enter the aircraft’s registration, clear the type field and add the aircraft. Tarmac then removes the remembered type for that registration.
  • Change a remembered type: add the aircraft with the new type.
  • Clear all of Fleet Memory: in Tarmac, open Settings and choose Wipe Fleet Memory Cache. Active aircraft are not affected.
  • Remove everything: deleting Tarmac removes its data from your iPhone.

Tarmac’s data may be included in backups of your iPhone made with iCloud Backup or on a computer, if you use them. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us, and deleting the app does not change backups that already exist.

Purchases

Tarmac is a one-time paid download from the App Store. It has no subscriptions and no in-app purchases. Apple processes the purchase under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Information Apple may provide to us

If you choose in your iPhone’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with crash reports and usage statistics about Tarmac. Apple controls what is collected and shared, and Apple’s privacy policy applies. Tarmac itself does not collect this information.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and we use them only to respond to you. Our email is handled by our email provider, Purelymail. We keep correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to deal with your enquiry and meet our legal obligations. Please do not send passwords, payment details or confidential operational records.

Children

Tarmac is a work tool and is not directed at children. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Tarmac changes how it handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact TinkerStack Pty Ltd at support@tinkerstack.au. This policy covers the Tarmac app; our website privacy notice covers this website.
